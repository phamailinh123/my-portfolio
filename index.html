About
my story

I didn't get into marketing because I liked ads. I got into it because I was fascinated by why people make choices — why one message lands and another disappears, why some brands feel like a place you want to belong to and others just blend into the noise.

Studying marketing taught me to ask better questions: Who is this really for? What are they actually feeling? What would make them stop and pay attention? I fell in love with the intersection of psychology, culture, and communication.

Then something changed. I started noticing that the best brands weren't just communicating well — they were building experiences. The website, the font, the way a page loaded — all of it was part of the story. That's when I started learning to code.

I'm not a developer yet. But I'm learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript because I believe that a marketer who can build is twice as powerful. I want to understand the medium I'm designing for — not just hand a brief across a desk and hope something good comes back.
